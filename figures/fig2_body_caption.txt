Figure 2 | Field audit on a clean fine ordinal: 5 of 7 measured scores carry ordering skill beyond their own declared primitive. Only the 7 scores measured on this ordinal are drawn here. The full 14-row audit, which adds the 7 rows that carry no value and prints the reason on each, is Figure A1. Conditional skill of each score after rank-residualizing on the primitive its authors declare, against the 12-stage Kimmel ordinal of GSE106474 (zebrafish whole embryos), Kendall tau_b, seed 42. Bars are point estimates; black whiskers are 95% bootstrap intervals and measure sampling precision of the point estimate, not the verdict. The dashed marker on each bar is that score's own decision floor: the 97.5th percentile of the estimator's measured null at that value's own n and its own score-primitive rank correlation rho, from the 200-seed null grid. Floors are per-row and not comparable across rows (+0.0115 to +0.0479 here) because they rise as n falls and vary with rho; there is no single threshold line. NCG, dpath and SLICE were measured on the shared seed-42 3,000-cell subsample, which raises their floors to 1.54-1.66 times the 39,505-cell floor at the same rho. CytoTRACE v1, SCENT SR, ORIGINS, NCG and dpath exceed their own floors. dpath clears under tau_b only: its weighted-tau value of +0.1914 sits below that kernel's own floor of +0.2417 at the same cell, so a rule requiring both kernels would read 4 of 7 here rather than 5 of 7. SLICE and CCAT do not; SLICE and CCAT are negative, and a negative value does not exceed a positive threshold — that is where the value sits, not a failed test. The rows not drawn here are in Figure A1: MCE, SPIDE and scEnergy blocked with no run artefact; StemSC measured, but on the sorted atlases rather than this ordinal; CytoTRACE 2 supervised and so outside the unsupervised class audited here; StemID and cmEntropy not measurements at all, each equal to a transcriptome-entropy primitive by construction.
