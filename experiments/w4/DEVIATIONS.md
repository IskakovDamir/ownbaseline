# W4 GATE 2: deviations from the locked pre-registration

The pre-registration (`2026-07-18-w4-independent-atlas-prereg.md`) and the GATE 1 atlas lock are named in the header of `scripts/w4_gate2_run.py`. Each entry below is a change made after the GATE 2 run, with its date, its reason and what it moved.

## 2026-10-09: C1 gene names merged across the two file groups

**What.** C1 (GSE117498) is now loaded with `load_c1(..., merge_names=True)`. Each broad-gate file's R `make.names` spelling (HLA.A) is renamed to the sorted files' HGNC spelling (HLA-A) before the eleven files are unioned. 4,137 names are merged, by the rule of `experiments/w5_new_rows/prepare_inputs.ds_gse117498h`; `experiments/w4_name_split/measure_split.py loader` checks that the two give the same matrix.

**Why.** The seven sorted-population files and the four broad-gate files spell 4,137 genes differently. A union by exact name split each into two columns, one zero in every broad-gate cell and the other zero in every sorted cell. Among them are all 13 MT- genes and 18 HLA genes, which are nodes of the STRING scaffold, so in the broad CD34+/CD164+ gate (6,343 of the 6,558 tier-4 cells) the scaffold scores and primitives were computed with about 8% of the cell's counts missing. This is a loading defect, not an analysis choice: the pre-registration locks the preprocessing (library-size normalisation, the 10-cell gene filter), the scaffold and the ordinal, and none of them changes.

**What moved.** `experiments/w4_name_split/NOTE.md` sets every value of the old and the new C1 run side by side. Under tau_b no verdict changes. For the CytoTRACE proxy the marginal gap goes from 0.229 to 0.181, the conditional skill from 0.232 [0.218, 0.247] to 0.195 [0.182, 0.210] and the score's AUROC from 0.272 to 0.269. The gene-count primitive's AUROC (0.378), its tau_b (-0.089), the per-population median detected genes and the 12,354 ranked cells do not change. C2 (GSE125970) is a single matrix with one naming convention and is unaffected.

**What did not change.** `load_c1`'s default is still `merge_names=False`. The paths that were registered or run on the split loader stay reproducible as they were: the w5 PREREG's GSE117498 line (its GSE117498h line is the merged one), `experiments/track4/code/gate2b_origins_c1c2.py`, `experiments/track4/code/gate2c_resolve.py` and `experiments/cytotrace_v1/ct_full_run.py`. None of those feeds a figure or the run record. `experiments/stemsc/stemsc_run.py` now passes `merge_names=True` as well; StemSC is identical in every cell under the merge, so its record is unchanged.
