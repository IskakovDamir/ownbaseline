Figure 2 | Field audit on a clean fine ordinal: 5 of 7 measured scores carry ordering skill beyond their own declared primitive. Conditional skill of each score after rank-residualizing on the primitive its authors declare, against the 12-stage Kimmel ordinal of GSE106474 (zebrafish whole embryos), Kendall tau_b, seed 42. Bars are point estimates; black whiskers are 95% bootstrap intervals and measure sampling precision of the point estimate, not the verdict. The dashed marker on each bar is that score's own decision floor: the 97.5th percentile of the estimator's measured null at that value's own n and its own score-primitive rank correlation rho, from the 200-seed null grid. Floors are per-row and not comparable across rows (+0.0115 to +0.0479 here) because they rise as n falls and vary with rho; there is no single threshold line. NCG, dpath and SLICE were measured on the shared seed-42 3,000-cell subsample, which raises their floors to 1.54-1.66 times the 39,505-cell floor at the same rho. CytoTRACE v1, SCENT SR, ORIGINS, NCG and dpath exceed their own floors. SLICE and CCAT do not; SLICE and CCAT are negative, and a negative value does not exceed a positive threshold — that is where the value sits, not a failed test. MCE, SPIDE and scEnergy are blocked and carry no value: the reason is printed on the row. StemSC was measured, but on the sorted atlases rather than this ordinal, so it has a row and no bar. CytoTRACE 2 is supervised and so sits outside the unsupervised class audited here; it has a row and no bar for the same reason. StemID and cmEntropy are not measurements — each equals a transcriptome-entropy primitive by construction, no run produces a number for them, and they appear only in the shaded band at the foot of the plot, which carries no scale. Values: CytoTRACE v1 +0.6531 [+0.6479, +0.6579] vs floor +0.0247 at n=39,505, rho=+0.4844; SCENT SR +0.4300 [+0.4248, +0.4350] vs floor +0.0311 at n=39,505, rho=+0.9305; ORIGINS +0.4019 [+0.3967, +0.4068] vs floor +0.0247 at n=39,505, rho=+0.4339; NCG +0.4015 [+0.3824, +0.4212] vs floor +0.0411 at n=3,000, rho=+0.3778; dpath +0.1202 [+0.0949, +0.1456] vs floor +0.0411 at n=3,000, rho=+0.4769; SLICE -0.1132 [-0.1352, -0.0915] vs floor +0.0479 at n=3,000, rho=+0.9317; CCAT -0.2209 [-0.2267, -0.2151] vs floor +0.0115 at n=39,505, rho=+0.9982.
