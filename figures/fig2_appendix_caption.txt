Figure A1 | Field audit on a clean fine ordinal: 6 of 9 measured scores carry ordering skill beyond their own declared primitive. This is the full audit behind Figure 3, which draws the 9 measured rows only; the 8 rows that carry no value are here, each with its reason. Conditional skill of each score after rank-residualizing on the primitive its authors declare, against the 12-stage Kimmel ordinal of GSE106474 (zebrafish whole embryos), Kendall tau_b, seed 42. Bars are point estimates; black whiskers are 95% bootstrap intervals and measure sampling precision of the point estimate, not the verdict. The dashed marker on each bar is that score's own decision floor: the 97.5th percentile of the estimator's measured null at that value's own n and its own score-primitive rank correlation rho, from the 200-seed null grid. Floors are per-row and not comparable across rows (+0.0115 to +0.0479 here) because they rise as n falls and vary with rho; there is no single threshold line. NCG, dpath and SLICE were measured on the shared seed-42 3,000-cell subsample, which raises their floors to 1.54-1.66 times the 39,505-cell floor at the same rho. CytoTRACE v1, SCENT SR, ORIGINS, NCG, dpath and StemFinder exceed their own floors. dpath clears under tau_b only: its weighted-tau value of +0.1914 sits below that kernel's own floor of +0.2417 at the same cell, so a rule requiring both kernels would read 5 of 9 here rather than 6 of 9. SLICE, CCAT and MCE do not; SLICE, CCAT and MCE are negative, and a negative value does not exceed a positive threshold. SPIDE and scEnergy are blocked and carry no value: the reason is printed on the row. StemSC was measured, but on the sorted atlases rather than this ordinal, so it has a row and no bar. CytoTRACE 2, mRNAsi and FitDevo are supervised or trained on labelled data and so sit outside the unsupervised class audited here; each has a row and no bar, and the trained rows print their own boundary results on the row. StemID and cmEntropy are not measurements: each equals a transcriptome-entropy primitive by construction, no run produces a number for them, and they appear only in the shaded band at the foot of the plot, which carries no scale.
